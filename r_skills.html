Data Skills with R

My Certificate in Applied Biostatistics and Public Health Data Science has provided me with a strong foundation in R, SAS, and SQL! This page demonstrates examples of my skills with R, based on a class assignment I produced in Data Science I.

New York City Restaurant Inspections Results

The New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (DOHMH) conducts annual restaurant inspections. This data is publicly available at NYC Open Data and is updated regularly. For the following products I used the DOHMH New York City Restaurant Inspection Results.

The dataset I used was accessed in October 2017 and I imported it from the course, Data Science I. I filtered the data to only include inspections performed in 2015 and those that received the inspection grades A, B, or C.

## Loads necessary packages

library(tidyverse)
library(janitor)
library(plotly)
library(p8105.datasets)
library(kableExtra)


## Initial cleaning to snake case 

## Selects columns of interest, filters only inspection data from 2015

rest_inspec_df=
  rest_inspec|>
  janitor::clean_names()|>
  drop_na(grade)|>
  filter(str_detect(inspection_date, "2015"))|>
  filter(grade != "P")|>
  select(-census_tract, -community_board, -council_district, -bin, -bbl, -nta, -action, -violation_description, -building, -street, -phone)

Interactive Visualizations

I produced the following products using Plot.ly! These graphs visualize the restaurant locations, frequency of letter grades, and distribution of inspection scores. To view these plots stimultaneously, visit the Sample Dashboard page.

Scatterplots

## Creates plotly scatterplot of restaurant locations using coordinates

rest_inspec_df |>
  drop_na(latitude, longitude)|>
  filter(latitude !="0")|>
  group_by(camis)|>
  mutate(
    text_label = str_c("Restaurant Identifier: ", camis, "\nName of Business: ", dba,
      "\nType of Cuisine: ", cuisine_description)
    )|>
  plot_ly(
    x = ~latitude, y = ~longitude, 
    text = ~text_label, color = ~boro, 
    type = "scatter", colors = "Dark2", mode = "markers", alpha = 0.5)|>
  layout(title = "2015 Inspected Restaurant Locations")

This is a scatter plot of NYC restaurant locations. The latitude is on the x-axis and the longitude is on the y-axis. As indicated in the legend, each borough is represented by a distinct color.

Bar Charts

## Creates plotly bar chart of inspection grades by borough

tab3|>
    pivot_longer(
    cols = A:C,
    names_to = "grade",
    values_to = "count"
  )|>
  plot_ly(x = ~grade, y = ~count, color = ~boro, type = "bar", colors = "Dark2")|>
  layout(title = "2015 Inspection Grades by Borough", xaxis = list(title = "Inspection Grades"), yaxis = list(title = "Frequency"))

This is a bar chart of the distribution of NYC restaurant inspection grades by borough. The inspection grades are on the x-axis and the frequency of each grade is on the y-axis. The bar colors indicate the respective boroughs, as indicated in the legend on the right.

Boxplots

## Creates boxplot of inspection scores by grade and grouped by borough

rest_inspec_df |>
  plot_ly(x = ~grade, y = ~score,
         color = ~boro, type = "box", colors = "Dark2")|>
    layout(boxmode = "group", title = "Distribution of 2015 Inspection Scores",
         xaxis = list(title ="Inspection Grade"),
         yaxis = list(title = "Score"))

This is a boxplot of the distribution of NYC restaurant inspection scores in 2015. Throughout restaurant inspections, scores were based on the presence of regulation violations and their severity. These scores were used to determine the inspection grade, with a higher score corresponding to a lower letter grade.
